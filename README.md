# privacy-policy-mero-mood
This page will have privacy policy of Mero Mood App (A Nepali Mental Health App.)

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how this Digital Mental Health App ("the App") handles your information. The App is designed to support your mental well-being through evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) techniques, and is available in both Nepali and English. Your privacy is a core part of how this App was built, and this policy explains exactly what that means in practice.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Data Collection and Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device. This includes, but is not limited to:

Thought records
Coping cards
Mindfulness session logs
Psychological assessment results
Activity tracker entries (sleep, daily activities, food habits)
App settings and preferences

None of this information ever leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it yourself.

No Cloud Storage

The App does not use cloud storage or remote servers to store your personal data. Everything you create stays on your phone or tablet. This means:

Complete privacy – no one can access your data without physical access to your device
Offline functionality – you can use the App anytime, even without an internet connection
Full data ownership – you retain complete control over your information at all times
No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your email address, username, or any other identifying information.

2. How We Protect Your Data

We take data security seriously and rely on the following measures:

Device Security: We recommend securing your device with a passcode, PIN, or biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition).
Secure Storage: The App uses your device's secure, built-in storage mechanisms to protect your data.
Minimal Permissions: The App only requests the minimum permissions necessary to function.
3. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with any third parties. Specifically:

No Analytics Companies – we do not send your usage data to analytics services
No Advertisers – we do not share your data with advertising networks
No Data Brokers – we do not sell or trade your information
No Research Institutions – we do not share your data for research purposes without your explicit consent

4. Your Rights and Control

Because your data belongs to you, you can at any time:

View your data through the App interface
Export your data whenever you want
Delete your data permanently

5. Exporting Your Data

You may export a full copy of your data at any time via Settings → Privacy Settings → Your Data → Export My Data. You can choose to save the exported file to your device storage, a cloud service, or share it via email.

Exported data is provided in JSON format, a standard, human-readable format that can be:

Opened with any text editor
Imported into spreadsheet tools like Excel or Google Sheets
Shared with healthcare professionals
Used for personal analysis

The export includes all activity tracker entries, thought records, coping cards, mindfulness session logs, assessment results, and app settings/preferences.

Important: Exported data may contain sensitive mental health information. Please store exported files securely and only share them with trusted healthcare professionals over secure, encrypted connections. There is no limit to how many times you may export your data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no data ever leaves the device or is transmitted to us, we have no ability to access, identify, or process data from any user, including minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
